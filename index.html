About Abhinav

Founder • Cybersecurity Researcher • Technologist

AR. Abhinav Ranjan (also known as ar.abhinavranjan) is a cybersecurity researcher, technologist, educator, and infrastructure architect. Known for his work in digital sovereignty and proactive defense, he focuses on securing modern technology systems and building awareness around emerging digital threats.

AR. Abhinav Ranjan is the Founder & CEO of Luminary Technicals, a multidisciplinary organization operating across digital infrastructure, technology development, research, cybersecurity, education, public-interest initiatives, and an expanding network of projects and ventures.

Recognized for early-career contributions to cybersecurity education and awareness, he has received honors including recognition from the Golden Book of World Records and Guinness World Records.

His work integrates technical engineering, ethical AI governance, and cyber jurisprudence to foster resilient and legally sound digital infrastructure.
